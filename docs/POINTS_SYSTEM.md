# MunchiesKK points system: brief for the content team

Written 12 Oct 2026 from the live app and database. Everything below is true
once the points update ships (see "Before you post" at the end). Numbers can
change: check with the owner before printing them on anything permanent.

## The one-line version

**Earn points on every order, bonus points for playing games, sharing and
inviting friends. Spend them on free food.**

## How customers EARN points

| How | Points | Rules to mention |
|---|---|---|
| **Order in the app** | **1 point per RM1 spent** | Counts the amount actually paid, after any promo. Orders must be placed in the app / munchieskk.my (counter sales rung up in Loyverse do not earn app points). |
| **Collect your order** | **+6** | Once per order, when the order is collected. |
| **Share your food** | **+20** | Tap Instagram / Facebook / TikTok after collecting. Once per order. |
| **Share + screenshot** | **40 in total** (+20 more) | Upload a screenshot of the public post (tag @munchieskk). Points are added after we approve it. Must be sent within 14 days of the order. |
| **Invite a friend** | **+150 for you, +30 for them** | Friend signs up with your link or code. The 150 is paid when their **first collected order of RM20 or more** is done. Max 5 friends per person. The friend's 30 points are paid when they sign up. |
| **Play Munch-Man** | up to +50 a game | One free play a day. Win = 50 pts, eat at least half the dots = 20 pts. |
| **Munch-Man daily streak** | +30 on day 3, +100 on day 7 | Play on consecutive days. |
| **Play T-Rex Runner / Speed Grab** | points for beating your own best score | Up to 20 points a day per game. |

**Game points have limits (say this clearly so nobody feels cheated):**
- Games give a **maximum of 150 bonus points a week** in total.
- **New accounts earn game points freely for their first 30 days.** After that, game points need at least one completed purchase on the account.
- Game days reset at **8:00 am Malaysia time**, not midnight.
- Order points, collection points, share points and referral points are **not** counted in the 150 limit.

**Rewards at checkout (new, only once the owner confirms it is live)**
Customers ordering in the app can tick "Use your points" on the payment page and pick one reward: it is added to the order at **RM0.00** (free item) or as a **free add-on on a burger**. The points are taken when the order is placed and **given back if the order is cancelled**. One reward per order, and each reward needs a minimum order value (set per reward by the owner, roughly RM10 / RM20 / RM40 by price tier). Rewards can only be added when the order is placed, not afterwards.

**How redeeming at the counter works (unchanged)**
1. Customer opens **Loyalty** in the app and taps Redeem. The points leave their balance straight away and they get a 6-character code.
2. At the counter, after they have paid for their order, they show the code or their name. Staff hand over the reward and tap Fulfil.
3. **A reward that is not collected within 14 days expires and the points go back to the customer.**

## Do NOT promise or mention

- **Tiers (Bronze / Silver / Gold).** They are not in the app. Do not mention them or any "tier perks".
- **A free burger.** No burger reward is live. Do not advertise one unless the owner adds it.
- **Points for counter (Loyverse) purchases.** Only app orders earn points for now.
- **Points that never expire.** Do not say "points never expire". Uncollected *rewards* expire after 14 days; the owner has not set an expiry on points themselves, so say nothing either way.
- **Unlimited game points.** Always mention the weekly limit if you mention games.
- **Specific rewards or prices not listed above.**
