# MunchiesKK Arcade Contest: explainer for the content team

Updated 13 Oct 2026. Items marked **(confirm live)** need the owner's OK before you announce them:
the contest only starts once the owner has set it up in the Admin panel and the app update is live.

## The idea in one line

**Play the three arcade games on munchieskk.my. Every month, the top 3 players win free food.
Hit the weekly goal for bonus points.**

## Two different things (please keep them separate)

| | **Loyalty points** | **Arcade Score** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Spend them on free food | Your game score. **Cannot be spent.** |
| How you get it | Orders, collecting, sharing, inviting friends | Playing T-Rex Runner, Speed Grab and Munch-Man |
| What it does | Buys rewards in the Prize Vault | Ranks you in the monthly contest and unlocks the weekly goal bonus |

A simple way to say it: **"Points buy food. Arcade Score wins prizes."**

## How Arcade Score works

- Your Arcade Score is your **best score in each of the three games, added together**.
  Example: best T-Rex 800 + best Speed Grab 500 + best Munch-Man 300 = **1,600**.
- Playing more only helps if you **beat your own best**. Replaying with a lower score changes nothing.
- Every game gives you **3 lives**. A crash costs a life and you carry on.
- You need to be **logged in** for scores to count. Guests can play for fun.
- Scores are **checked by the server**. Impossible scores are not counted.

## The weekly goal

- Reach **1,000 Arcade Score in a week** (Monday to Sunday, Malaysia time) and get **+25 loyalty points**. Once a week.
- Because the three games add together, most regular players can reach it. That is on purpose.
- New accounts qualify freely for their first 30 days. After that, an account needs at least one collected order.
- The owner can change the goal and the bonus. Always check the numbers with the owner before printing them.

## The monthly contest **(confirm live)**

- **One contest a month**, running the whole calendar month: from the 1st at 12:00 am to the end of the month (Malaysia time).
- The **top 3 Arcade Scores win free food**. The plan is:
  - **1st: a free burger**
  - **2nd: free fries**
  - **3rd: a free drink**
  The owner can change the prizes for any month. Confirm before announcing.
- **Rules to state clearly:**
  - Ranking = best score in each game, added together. A **tie goes to the player who reached the score first**.
  - **One account per phone number.** If two accounts share a number, only the better one counts.
  - A **minimum score** applies (300 by default) so that a single quick try does not win.
  - Winners are picked **automatically when the month ends**.
  - Winners get a **claim code in the app**, shown at the counter. The plan is **no purchase needed** and **14 days to claim** (confirm both).
  - **Staff check the top players** in person before handing over a prize.

## What players see in the app

- The Arcade page shows the current contest: the prizes, how much time is left, **their own rank and score**, and how far they are from the podium.
- A progress bar shows the weekly goal.
- After a contest ends, the app shows **first names only** of the winners. There is **no public full leaderboard**. Please do not post one.
- Winners see a gold card with their claim code.

Sample captions (edit freely, keep to the facts above):

- "Weekly goal: 1,000 Arcade Score = +25 loyalty points. Three games, one score."

## Please do NOT

- Say that games give loyalty points for every win or personal best. Only the **weekly goal** gives points (and Munch-Man keeps its own win and streak points, listed in the points brief).
- Promise a prize that is not confirmed for that month, or say "free burger" for everyone.
- Post a full leaderboard, full names, phone numbers or scores of other players.
- Say "no purchase needed" or "14 days to claim" until the owner confirms it.
- Call it a lucky draw. It is a **skill contest**: the best scores win.
- Encourage cheating, bots or multiple accounts. Cheated scores are not counted and can lose the prize.

## Quick FAQ for replies and comments

- **Do I need to order food to play?** No. Playing is free for everyone who has an account. (After the first 30 days, the weekly goal bonus needs at least one collected order.)
- **Can I use two accounts?** One account per phone number counts. Please do not.
- **What if there is a tie?** The player who reached the score first wins.
- **When are the winners announced?** When the month ends, once the owner confirms them.
- **Where do I collect my prize?** At the counter, with the claim code from the app.
- **Why did my score not count?** Make sure you are logged in. Scores that are impossible for the time played are not counted.
