# Automation ideas (saved 19 Nov 2026)

From a social-media post about "AI agents every restaurant needs". Rated for MunchiesKK: a pickup burger kitchen with no tables. This is a wish list, not a plan. **Nothing here is built.** Launch comes first (real payments, stock, staff drills; see `SOFT_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`).

| Idea from the post | Fits MunchiesKK? | What it would mean for us | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reservations (WhatsApp, table holds) | No | We have no tables. Skip. | Never |
| One queue for all orders (Grab, own site, till) | Partly, already half there | App orders, the till (Loyverse) and the GrabFood tab already reach one place. Grab is typed in by hand; there is no easy Grab link for a small shop. Missing: tell the customer "ready for pickup". | After launch |
| No-shows (confirm, release the table) | Different form | Our versions already exist: unpaid orders expire after 15 minutes and stock returns; unused rewards expire. Possible extra: nudge customers whose paid order is not collected. | After launch |
| Review requests the morning after | Yes, cheap | After an order is collected, ask how it was. **Do not filter who gets the Google link** (see warning). | After launch |
| Promos for a quiet day | Partly | Double points Tue/Wed/Thu already does this. Messaging customers needs their permission and a paid WhatsApp business tool. | Later |
| Stock counts and supplier orders | Yes | Low-stock alerts exist. Drafting supplier orders needs the recipes (stock by recipe step). | After recipes |
| Rosters | No (team too small) | Skip for now. | Later |
| One daily summary page for the owner | Yes, best value | One page each morning: sales, orders, top items, low stock, late payments, stock count differences, what needs you. Admin analytics and reports already hold most of the numbers. | First after launch |

## Warning: review gating
The post says 5 stars go to Google and anything under 4 comes to the owner privately. Google's review rules forbid selectively asking only happy customers for public reviews, and reviews can be removed or the listing penalised. Safe version: ask **everyone** for feedback, give **everyone** the Google link, and also offer a private "tell us what went wrong" option for anyone who wants it.

## Suggested order after opening
1. Daily owner summary.
2. "Your order is ready" message (WhatsApp click-to-chat link or a web notification).
3. Feedback prompt after collection (not gated).
4. Supplier order drafts, once recipe stock exists.
Anything that messages customers needs a clear opt-in and must be mentioned in the Privacy Policy.

## Notes
- Most of these are plain automations, not "AI". Start with simple rules; add AI only where it saves real time.
- The post ends by asking people to comment a word to receive a "map". That is a marketing funnel for the poster's own service; no action needed.
