# MunchiesKK: how the site, points and prizes work (brief for finance)

Written 14 Oct 2026. The shop has not opened, so there is no sales data yet.
Everything below is a rule in the app unless marked as an assumption. Item costs are for finance to fill in.
Names of the points may change (see the end); this brief uses "Order Points" and "Arcade Score".

## 1. What the site is

munchieskk.my is the shop's ordering site (burgers, fries, drinks, sliders and packs). Customers create an account, order in the app,
and collect at the shop. Staff move each order through Placed, Ready and Collected in the admin screen. Orders can be cancelled
until they are collected. The site also has a loyalty scheme and three small games ("the arcade").

Two kinds of numbers exist, and they must not be confused:

| Name in this brief | What it is | Can it be spent? |
|---|---|---|
| **Order Points** | The loyalty balance. Earned mostly by ordering. Spent on free items. | **Yes.** This is the only thing that costs us food. |
| **Arcade Score** | A game score, best score in each of 3 games added together. | **No.** It only decides a weekly bonus and a monthly contest. |

## 2. How Order Points are earned

| Source | Order Points | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Ordering | **1 point per RM1** of food paid | Worked out when the order is placed, **paid only when the order is collected**. A cancelled order earns nothing. Only orders placed in the app earn points; counter sales rung up in the till do not. |
| Collection bonus | **+6 per collected order** | Flat amount, paid with the order points. |
| Double points days | **x2 on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday** | By the day the order is placed (Malaysia time). The owner can switch it off, change the multiplier (1 to 5) or the days. |
| Rank bonus | **+5% / +8% / +12%** of the order points | Ranks Pro / Master / Ultimate, from lifetime spend on collected orders: Regular RM200, Pro RM850, Master RM1,500, Ultimate RM3,000. |
| Sharing a meal | **+20**, or **+40 in total** with an approved screenshot | Once per order, collected orders only. |
| Inviting a friend | **+150 to the inviter, +30 to the friend** | Paid when the friend's **first collected order is RM20 or more**. Each person can earn this for a maximum of 5 friends. |
| Arcade weekly goal | **+25 per week** | When a player's Arcade Score reaches 1,000 in a Monday to Sunday week. After the account's first 30 days, the player must also have at least one collected order. |

Games pay **no** points per play. There is no daily game payout.

## 3. How Order Points are spent

At checkout the customer picks one reward. It is added to the order at RM0 and the points are deducted. If the order is cancelled, the points are returned.
Rules: **one reward per order**, a **minimum order of RM20** (RM30 for the platter until 31 Dec 2026, then under review),
and a monthly limit by rank (Rookie 1, Regular 2, Pro 3, Master 4, Ultimate 5 rewards a month).

| Reward | Order Points | Our cost (finance to fill in) |
|---|---|---|
| Free Cheese (add-on) | 50 | RM ___ |
| Free Egg (add-on) | 60 | RM ___ |
| Free Fries | 100 | RM ___ |
| Ice BB | 350 | RM ___ |
| Free Mushy2 | 800 | RM ___ |
| Kawan MONSTA platter | 3,000 | RM ___ |

**Expiry:** points fade only after 12 months with no activity (no order, game score or reward claim), at 25% of the balance a month.
Any order stops it.

## 4. The arcade (games) and the monthly contest

Three games: T-Rex Runner, Speed Grab, Munch-Man. Each run adds to Arcade Score. Only a player's **best score in each game** counts, and each game counts **at most 3,000**.
The highest possible Arcade Score is 9,000.

- **Weekly goal:** 1,000 Arcade Score in a week gives **+25 Order Points**, once a week. At most about 100 points in a new account's first month.
- **Monthly contest:** the top 3 players for the calendar month win a **free burger, free fries and a free drink**.
  The first contest runs 1 Nov to 1 Dec 2026, before the shop opens.
  A player needs at least 300 Arcade Score to qualify. Ties go to whoever reached the score first, and one account per phone number counts.
  Winners show a claim code at the counter and must **place an order of RM10 or more** to claim, within **60 days** of winning. Unclaimed prizes cost nothing.

## 5. Where the cost comes from

1. **Free rewards** bought with Order Points (section 3). This is the main cost and it scales with sales.
2. **Contest prizes**: at most 3 items a month (section 4). The content team estimated RM19 a month. Is that our cost or the menu price?
3. **Referrals**: 180 points per successful invite (150 + 30).
4. **Weekly goal**: up to 25 points a week per active player.

No cash is ever paid out. Points have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Every reward needs an order of at least RM20 (RM10 for contest prizes), which is also revenue.

## 6. Illustrative scale (assumptions, not data)

200 active customers, 2 orders each a month, average order RM25: sales RM10,000. If 40% of orders fall on double days, average points per order are about 35 plus 6 = 41.
400 orders gives about 16,400 points. If every point were spent on Free Fries (100 points each) that is about 164 fries; at the food cost finance supplied (RM1.60 a fries) that is about RM262, or about 2.6% of sales (about 3.4% with oil and gas).
In practice not all points are spent and some fade, so this is a ceiling for that scenario.

## 7. Controls the owner has, without a developer

The double-points days and multiplier; the weekly goal size and points; each reward's point price and stock; the contest prizes, dates, minimum score, claim minimum and claim window; and
whether each reward is available. The flat +6 collection bonus and the 3,000-per-game cap need a developer to change.

## 8. Things I'd ask finance to look at

1. **The flat +6 bonus** favours small orders: a RM10 order earns 16 points (1.6 per RM), a RM40 order earns 46 (1.15 per RM).
2. **Double points Tue to Thu**: roughly doubles the reward rate on three days a week. These are the quietest days.
3. **Referral cost** of 180 points, against a first order of at least RM20.
4. How should unspent points be recorded in the accounts?
5. What reward cost as a share of sales is acceptable?
