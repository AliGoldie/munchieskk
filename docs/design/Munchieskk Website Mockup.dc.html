Our Story

Not your usual
burger joint.

Munchies KK started as a small Sabahan stall with one idea: fuse local flavour into the American burger, made fully halal, fully homemade. Every sauce — from sambal tuhau to our creamy mushroom blend — is handcrafted in our own kitchen, no shortcuts, no store-bought bottles.

Today we serve Kota Kinabalu and Penampang, still cooking every patty to order.
